    Controls
    WASD
    Move. Hold Shift to sprint.
    L and R markers
    Two markers always track the best thing to grab on the left and right of your view. Point the crosshair at something to grab exactly that instead.
    Left click
    Fire the left grapple. Hold to reel yourself in.
    Right click
    Fire the right grapple. Hold to reel yourself in. Two ropes at once: hang between anchors.
    Shift · Left Alt
    Let go of the left · right grapple.
    Q · E
    Pull what the left · right grapple caught (a crate, bomb, the idol, a friend or bot on the left one) toward you. Then F to throw or bat it.
    Two ropes, reeled straight
    Hold both buttons to reel in until you hang almost straight: they merge into a tightrope you can walk on.
    Tab
    Hold to see the standings.
    Shift (gliding)
    Dive straight down; WASD tilts the dive. Let go to swoop out and up. Ram someone mid-dive to dive bomb them.
    Space
    Jump and double jump. While hooked, let go of both grapples with a boost.
    Wheel · R
    Zoom the camera · respawn.
    G
    Use your item: grab rainbow ? boxes for a rocket boost, bomb, homing comet, shield or spark magnet. Bombs blow holes in towers.
    F
    Shove whoever is right in front of you. With a red bat pickup it becomes a home-run swing.
    Glider
    Grab a cyan glider pickup, then hold Space while falling (60 s). Hold Shift to dive.
    Enter · V
    Type in chat, or hold V and talk to send voice as text. Characters answer back in text. Scroll the chat panel to see earlier messages.
    T
    Restart the speedrun. The timer starts on your first move.
    Esc
    Pause. Click the game to capture the mouse again.
    Tap
    (Swing scheme) Fire a hook where you tap. Tap again for a second rope.
    Swipe ↓ / ↑
    While hooked: swipe down to pull yourself in, up to let go. Sideways turns the camera.
    Pull
    While hooked onto something loose or someone, drag it toward you.
    Left thumb
    Drag to move.
    Right thumb
    Drag to look around.
    Hook
    Hold to grapple at the crosshair, release to let go.
    Reel · Jump
    Reel in while hooked. Jump while hooked to launch off.
    Hit
    Shove anyone close in front of you. Grab a red bat for a home-run swing.
    Glider
    After a cyan pickup, hold Jump while falling to glide.
    Restart run
    Resets the island. The timer starts on your first move.
