    Skyhook Isles

    An island of stone pillars and floating rocks. Swing between them with your grapple and collect the 15 sparks. Each one sends a beam of light into the sky.

    WASD
    Move. Hold Shift to sprint.
    Mouse
    Look. Hold the left or right button (or E) to grapple where you aim.
    Shift
    While hooked, reel in. Hook a crate and reel to yank it.
    Space
    Jump and double jump. While hooked, let go with a boost.
    Wheel · R
    Zoom the camera · respawn.
    Glider
    Grab a cyan glider pickup, then hold Space while falling. Look down to dive, up to float.
    T
    Restart the speedrun. The timer starts on your first move.
    Esc
    Pause. Click the game to capture the mouse again.
    Left thumb
    Drag to move.
    Right thumb
    Drag to look around.
    Hook
    Hold to grapple at the crosshair, release to let go.
    Reel · Jump
    Reel in while hooked. Jump while hooked to launch off.
    Glider
    After a cyan pickup, hold Jump while falling to glide.
    Restart run
    Resets the island. The timer starts on your first move.
